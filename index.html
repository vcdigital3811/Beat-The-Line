Beat the Line

Tap the lane the instant a note crosses the line. Nail the timing, build the streak.

  1. Notes fall down 4 lanes toward the white hit line near the bottom.
  2. Press D, F, J, K (or tap the lane on screen) the moment a note crosses the line.
  3. Closer to the line = better judgment: PERFECT, GOOD, or MISS.
  4. Consecutive hits build a combo streak that multiplies your score.
  5. Round lasts 45 seconds. Keep the streak alive for the highest score.
